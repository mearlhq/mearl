### Browser control

- Use `mearl browser_control --browser <browserId>` for handoff and resume. Ordinary browsers select their sole unfinished session automatically; specify `tabId` or `--session` when multiple sessions exist.
- External Providers can declare handoff, resume and task completion operations. Ego Space lifecycle uses the unified command, including retaining selected result pages on completion, without a separate `mearl-ego` executable.
- Updated CLI, MCP and website control documentation. Unified Ego control requires `@mearl/provider-ego` 0.1.1+ and Mearl 3.0.7+.
