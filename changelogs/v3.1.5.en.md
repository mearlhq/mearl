### Tracking and device preview

- Selecting Mearl in DevTools hides the embedded debug panel and its controls for the inspected tab, freeing the space in the device preview. Switching away restores the saved open or closed preference.
- The heatmap button appears based on the page’s SPM A segment. Click the flame to open or close the metric selector. The overlay displays the values of all selected metrics in selection order, separated by `/`.
- Click the page’s AB value to copy it in full. The heatmap sign-in prompt and link fit on one row in narrow windows.
